SKZLX Foundation - upload instructions

Extract this ZIP, then upload its contents to the root of skzlx.com.
Replace the old index.html. Keep the icons folder beside it.
You do not need to upload this README.
The music and downloadable ZIP remain embedded in index.html.
Do not extract or replace the embedded download with this website package.

The companion files supply the public social preview image, browser/home-screen
icons, web app manifest, robots.txt and sitemap.xml. Keep these files public.
The page and its audio/download still function if you open index.html locally;
external platforms need the companion files published to show the intended image.

Metadata targets https://skzlx.com/. If you use a different domain, update the
canonical, sharing URLs, structured data, robots.txt and sitemap.xml together.

After uploading:
- Visit https://skzlx.com/ and check Play/Pause and DOWNLOAD.
- Confirm /social-preview.png, /robots.txt and /sitemap.xml open publicly.
- Submit https://skzlx.com/sitemap.xml in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster
  Tools if you have verified ownership. Verification tokens were not invented.
- Existing preview images may be cached until the platform refreshes the URL.

Search engines, social apps and AI services choose what to crawl, index and show.
The metadata and structured data describe the real page; they do not guarantee
rankings, inclusion, rich results, or previews in every app. robots.txt allows all
crawlers, including AI crawlers that honor these rules; server/CDN rules may differ.
Home-screen availability depends on browser support. No offline service worker
was added. Apple Passwords/passkeys require an actual sign-in system; this page
has none. There are no invented Xbox/PlayStation/AI indexing meta tags.

Validated locally: ZIP/audio payloads and functional body unchanged, valid JSON
and XML, and all packaged icon/preview paths present. Live crawling, server
headers and platform rendering must be checked after you upload.
